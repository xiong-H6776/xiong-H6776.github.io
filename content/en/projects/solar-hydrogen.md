## Main Work

Building on the advantages of the AMSP biomass-to-hydrogen mechanism, I contributed to the coupled design of a two-tank thermal storage system and the development of a dedicated integrated solar hydrogen-production reactor. Solar collection and reaction functions were integrated to improve hydrogen-production efficiency.

## Technologies

- Thermodynamic simulation with COMSOL
- 3D modeling with SolidWorks

## Project Recognition

- First Prize at the School of Energy and Power Engineering's Tianyuan Environmental Protection Innovation Cup
- University-level Excellence Award in the National College Student Energy Conservation and Emission Reduction Competition
- Second Prize in the National College Student Renewable Energy Competition
